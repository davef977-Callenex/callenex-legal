# Privacy Policy

**Callenex LLC**
**Last Updated: December 31, 2024**

## Overview

Callenex ("we," "our," or "us") is a call blocking application that protects you from scam and robocalls. We built Callenex with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

## Our Privacy Commitment

**We do NOT:**
- Listen to your phone calls
- Access your contacts
- Read your call history
- Sell your data to third parties
- Share your personal information with advertisers

**Call blocking happens entirely on your device** using Apple's Call Directory Extension. We provide the list of numbers to block — your iPhone handles the actual blocking. We never see who calls you.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- **Email address** — Used for login and account recovery
- **Phone number** — Used for account verification

### App Usage Data
To provide and improve our service, we collect:
- **App version and build number** — To ensure compatibility
- **Extension status** — Whether call blocking is enabled
- **Sync timestamps** — When your block list was last updated
- **Device type and iOS version** — For troubleshooting

### User-Generated Data
- **Custom blocked numbers** — Numbers you manually add to your block list
- **Reported scam numbers** — Numbers you report to our community database
- **Feedback responses** — Your responses to in-app satisfaction prompts

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Authenticate your account
- Sync threat intelligence to your device
- Improve our scam detection database
- Respond to support requests
- Send critical service updates (rare)

We do **not** use your information for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Storage and Security

- Your account data is stored securely using Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- Authentication is handled by AWS Cognito with industry-standard encryption
- All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- We do not store passwords — only secure password hashes

## Third-Party Services

Callenex uses the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Their Privacy Policy |

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties.

## Data Retention

- **Account data** — Retained while your account is active
- **Usage logs** — Retained for 90 days for troubleshooting
- **Reported numbers** — Retained indefinitely to protect the community
