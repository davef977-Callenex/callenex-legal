## Description of Service

Callenex is a call blocking application that helps protect users from scam and robocalls by:
- Maintaining a database of known scam phone numbers
- Syncing this database to your device
- Using Apple's Call Directory Extension to block matching calls

## Important Limitations

### No Guarantee of Protection

Callenex significantly reduces unwanted calls, but **we cannot guarantee that all scam calls will be blocked**. New scam numbers emerge constantly, and some may reach you before they are added to our database.

### How Blocking Works

- Callenex uses Apple's native Call Directory Extension
- Blocking occurs at the iOS system level
- **We do not intercept, record, or listen to any calls**
- Calls from numbers in your Contacts will not be blocked by iOS, regardless of our database
