Your Privacy Matters
At Callenex, we respect your right to control your personal data. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Data Deletion

To delete your Callenex account and all associated data, please send an email to:

Email: support@callenex.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request
Include: The email address associated with your Callenex account

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving your deletion request, we will permanently delete the following data:

  • Account Information: Email address, phone number, and account credentials
  • Authentication Data: Login tokens and session information
  • App Activity: Usage data, preferences, and settings
  • User Profile: Any profile information you've provided
  • Cloud Sync Data: Any data synced to our servers

Data Retention Period

Once we receive your deletion request:

  • Processing Time: We will process your request within 30 days
  • Confirmation: You will receive an email confirmation once deletion is complete
  • Permanent Deletion: All data will be permanently deleted from our systems
⚠️ Important: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, you will need to create a new account if you wish to use Callenex again.

What Data We Retain

For legal and compliance purposes, we may retain certain limited information:

  • Transaction Records: If you made any purchases (required for tax compliance)
  • Legal Compliance: Data required by law to be retained
  • Anonymized Analytics: Aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics

Alternative: Account Deactivation

If you'd like to temporarily stop using Callenex without deleting your data, you can:

  • Simply uninstall the app from your device
  • Your data will remain inactive on our servers
  • You can reinstall and log back in at any time
